The car

Marigold orange, and a bit tired

I bought this Beetle because it made me smile in traffic. I drove it when the weather was kind and put a cover on it when it was not. The cover lost.

The nose and the rear wing have rust, and both are in the photos on purpose. It starts, it stops, the heater is optimistic, and there is no air conditioning, because it is 1974.

You get two keys, a folder of old invoices, and a front pair of tyres from last spring. I am selling it because I need a back seat. Come and hear it run before you decide.
